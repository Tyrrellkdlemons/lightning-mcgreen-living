## Accepted guidance and boundaries

Use state-specific referrals, source dates, closed-program labels, document-preparation checklists, direct provider confirmation, and clear search versus application buttons. Keep urgent support accessible without requiring personal-data entry. Directory records are not current units or open intake.

Do not create legal-deadline calculators, determine an individual's housing eligibility, assert live vacancies/funding/parking rights, or copy property photography without evidence and rights. Source/gallery links and maps provide views without implying verified unit photos. The notebook is a research workflow; this release does not embed a live Gemini chat or expose the private notebook publicly.
